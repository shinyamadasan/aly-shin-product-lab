## Brand Principles

- Earn trust through consistency.
- Make quality visible in daily operations.
- Treat hospitality as a system, not a mood.
- Keep the brand warm, clear, and dependable.
- Document decisions so the brand can scale without losing itself.

## Voice

The Aly & Pon voice should feel:

- Warm.
- Clear.
- Confident.
- Honest.
- Welcoming.

Avoid language that feels overly corporate, gimmicky, or vague.
