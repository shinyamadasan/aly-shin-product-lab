# Knowledge: Food Regulatory Awareness (Philippine FDA context)

Referenced by `specialists/food-science-quality-specialist.md`. **This is general awareness
content, not legal or regulatory advice.** Regulations change and have real exceptions and
local-government layers on top of them — verify anything load-bearing against the current
official source (FDA Philippines, and the relevant city/barangay business-permit office) before
treating it as settled. Aly & Shin should confirm specifics with an actual official source or
professional before launch, not rely on this file alone.

Business context: PHP pricing and Philippine retail brands throughout this app's data point to
this being a Philippines-based home operation, so this file is written with FDA Philippines (Food
and Drug Administration) in mind, not the US FDA.

## Why this matters at the current stage, and why it might not yet

`PRODUCT_LAB_CONTEXT.md` places this business firmly in home-based product testing, pre-launch —
not yet operating as a registered commercial food business. Many formal registration and
labeling requirements are typically tied to commercial sale at scale, not small-batch home
testing or proofing among a small circle. That said, "not yet required" is different from "never
relevant" — the categories below are worth being aware of now so they don't become a surprise
right before a real preorder/launch.

## General categories worth being aware of (verify current specifics before acting)

- **Food business registration/licensing** — commercial food sale in the Philippines generally
  involves both a local government business permit and FDA product/business registration once
  operating beyond small home-based testing. When this business moves toward real preorder/
  launch, confirm what tier of registration actually applies at that point.
- **Labeling** — nutrition information, ingredient listing, allergen declaration, and
  manufacture/expiry dating are common requirements once a food product is sold commercially with
  packaging. A product without any of this on its packaging is a gap to flag once launch is
  actually being considered, even if it's fine during home proofing.
- **Allergen disclosure** specifically — several ingredients already in this app's data (dairy,
  egg, coffee, cocoa/chocolate) are common allergens; disclosure matters regardless of formal
  registration status, simply as an honest customer-safety practice.
- **Home-based/cottage food provisions** — some jurisdictions distinguish small home-kitchen
  operations from full commercial food manufacturing, sometimes with different or deferred
  requirements. Whether and how this applies here is exactly the kind of thing to confirm with an
  actual current source, not assume from general knowledge.
